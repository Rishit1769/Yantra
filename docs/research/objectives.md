# Research Objectives

Research features are experimental proposals, not roadmap commitments or existing functionality. They must remain behind explicit configuration, use reproducible protocols, and be compared against simple baselines before adoption into defaults.

Objectives: context allocation under token constraints; model routing under capability/cost/latency constraints; evidence-guided recovery; and candidate skill learning. Shared requirements include dataset provenance, pinned code/config/model metadata, privacy review, ablations, uncertainty reporting, and publication of negative results. See [benchmarks](benchmarks.md) and [reproducibility](reproducibility.md).
