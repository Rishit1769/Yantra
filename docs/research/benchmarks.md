# Benchmarks

YANTRA publishes no benchmark results today. A benchmark report must define task completion rubric, dataset license/provenance, version and split, baseline implementations, model/provider/deployment, sampling parameters, tool versions, budgets, environment, concurrency, retries, scoring code, sample size, failed-run handling, uncertainty, and exclusions. Compare matched conditions and include cost/latency distributions rather than a single favorable aggregate.

Safety metrics include policy violation rate and duplicate-effect rate; define denominator and adjudication. Do not compare unlike provider capabilities or report unsupported precision. Preserve raw manifests while minimizing sensitive payloads. Negative and inconclusive results are retained.
